Frequently Asked Questions (John Deere 4044M / 4052M / 4066M (400E / 440R Loader))

How much rear ballast do I need for John Deere 4044M / 4052M / 4066M (400E / 440R Loader) (2,509 lb loader)?

To safely lift 2,509 lbs at the loader pivot pins without overloading the front axle or lifting a rear tire on uneven ground, hang 1,810 lbs on the 3-point hitch (approx. 70%–75% of loader capacity) AND fill both rear tires with 84.0 gallons of liquid ballast (+907 lbs Beet Juice or +706 lbs Washer Fluid).

How many 80-lb bags of concrete fit inside a 3-point ballast box for 1,810 lbs?

A standard Category 1 steel ballast box weighs ~135 lbs empty and holds ~5.0 to 5.3 cubic feet. Adding 21 bags of 80-lb ready-mix concrete (+1680 lbs) brings the total 3-point hitch counterweight to 1,815 lbs.

How much can John Deere 4044M / 4052M / 4066M (400E / 440R Loader) lift with clamp-on pallet forks?

Manufacturer loader ratings (2,509 lbs) are measured at the bucket pivot pins. Because clamp-on pallet forks shift the load's center of gravity 24 inches forward of the bucket edge, safe lifting capacity is ~58% of pivot capacity = 1,460 lbs (with full rear ballast attached).
